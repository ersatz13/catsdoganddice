Base hand scores are compressed (most best hands are 3–4 points). That makes bonus cards and color bonuses the dominant score source.
Per‑die bonuses are huge: objective +1 per die (up to +5) already beats most base hands. Add all‑same‑color (+1) + “Who Let the Dawgs Out?” (+2 per copy) + Psychedelic (+1 per purple) and color‑focused builds can blow past hand strength.

Snuggle Buddies (+2 per color pair per copy) and One Dawg Wolf Pack (+1 per solitary color per copy) can add 4–10 points on their own.
Street Dawg mismatch: app.py awards +5 points per copy if not the score leader, but game.py still describes +1 if fewer kibbles than leader. The +5 version is a massive swing and likely dominates end‑round scoring.
Suggested balance adjustments (pick any you like)

Cap or soften per‑die bonuses: e.g., objective bonus capped at +3, or +1 total if you have ≥3 objective dice (instead of +1 per die).
Diminishing returns on duplicates: first copy full effect, extra copies only +1 point each (or cap at 2 copies).
Re‑scale base hand scores: e.g., Full house 4, Large straight 5, Four‑kind 6, Five‑kind 7, so hand strength matters more than flat bonuses.
Tone down Street Dawg: align with game.py (+1) or keep +5 but only once per round and not per copy.

Economy smoothing: kibbles are generous (4/3/2/2 with no splitting), so dog stacks happen fast. Consider reducing kibbles or raising dog cost if you want slower power growth.
